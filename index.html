Slope Slide Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

Slope Slide is a math puzzle game for Android: you type equations, curves appear on a graph, and marbles roll along them to collect stars. This page explains what information the game uses, why, and who else handles it. The game is made for players of all ages, including children.

The short version

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • We do not sell any information, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • Players who say they are under 13 only get ads that are non-personalized and marked as child-directed.
  • Links that leave the game (such as this page) sit behind a grown-up check.

Information kept on your phone

  • Game progress and settings: stars earned, Blitz best scores, equations you were working on, hint counts, and settings such as sound and vibration.
  • Birth year: the first time you open the game, it asks for your birth year. This is used only to choose the right kind of ads (see below). It stays on your phone and is never sent to us or to cloud save.
  • Purchases: whether you bought "Remove ads", so the game works offline. Google Play is checked again whenever you are online.

Android's own backup (Auto Backup) may copy this save file to your Google account, so your progress comes back if you reinstall or move to a new phone. You can turn this off in your phone's backup settings.

Services from Google that the game uses

  • Google Play Games Services: you sign in with Play Games so your progress can be saved to the cloud (Saved Games). The saved data is your game progress only: no birth year and no purchase details. Google handles your Play Games profile under its own policy.
  • Google AdMob (ads): free players see banner ads on menu screens and occasional full-screen ads between levels. You can also choose to watch an ad for an extra hint. AdMob may collect device information, such as the advertising ID and IP address, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the game asks for your consent first using Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later in Settings.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports): if the game crashes or sign-in or cloud save fails, a report is sent so we can fix the problem. It includes the error, the phone model, Android version, app version, the name of the screen you were on, and a random installation ID. It does not include your name, email, birth year or equations.
  • Google Play Billing: if you buy "Remove ads", Google Play handles the payment. We never see your payment details.

You can read Google's policies here: Google Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Firebase privacy information.

Children's privacy

Slope Slide is suitable for children. When a player gives a birth year that makes them under 13:

  • ad requests are marked as child-directed (under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA) and as under the age of consent (for European rules such as GDPR);
  • ads are non-personalized and limited to content rated for general audiences;
  • the game never asks for personal information such as a name or email address.

The birth year only changes ad settings; it is not used for anything else. A parent or guardian who has questions, or who wants information removed, can contact us at the address below.

Your choices

  • Buying "Remove ads" turns off banner and full-screen ads. Watching an ad for a hint is always optional.
  • You can review your ad consent choice in the game's Settings, where it applies.
  • You can reset your phone's advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your phone's Google settings.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on your phone. Cloud save data can be deleted from your Play Games settings.

Keeping information safe

Information sent to Google's services is encrypted in transit. We keep crash reports only as long as needed to fix problems, and we do not combine them with any other information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
